Data Protection

Know what is sensitive and stop it walking out.

Classification and data loss prevention that people can live with, covering email, cloud storage, endpoints and the screen itself.

Discuss Data Protection ▸Open in the interactive menu

Who it's for

  • Businesses handling confidential documents, customer data or intellectual property
  • Organisations whose data loss prevention (DLP) tool blocks too much or too little
  • Teams that need a data classification scheme people will actually use

Problems it helps with

  • No agreed way to label what is confidential
  • DLP rules so noisy that people work around them
  • Sensitive files shared too widely in cloud storage
  • Documents photographed or screenshotted off the screen

Scope and deliverables

A classification scheme, DLP policies tuned against real false-positive rates, and access reviews for your sensitive repositories, rolled out with a plan for telling staff.

What we do

  • Data classification scheme and labelling
  • DLP policy design and tuning
  • Screen-capture protection for classified documents
  • Access review for sensitive repositories

What you get

  • Classification policy
  • DLP rule set with measured false positives
  • Roll-out and comms plan

How an engagement works

  1. Conversation

    You tell us what's going on and what you need. We ask questions to understand your environment and constraints.

  2. Written scope

    We agree in writing what is in and out of scope, what you'll receive and when, before any work starts.

  3. The work

    Carried out as agreed. Anything urgent is raised with you as soon as it's found, not saved for the report.

  4. Report and walkthrough

    Findings in plain language, ranked by what to fix first, and a walkthrough with your team.

Questions

Do we need to buy a DLP product?

Not necessarily. Many businesses already own DLP features in Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace that are switched off or untuned.

How do you keep false positives down?

Rules are measured in a monitoring-only phase before anything is blocked, and tuned against what they actually catch.

What about photos of the screen?

Traditional DLP can't see a phone camera. That gap is why we build Glint, our own product for it.
