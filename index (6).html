Compliance & GRC

Pass the audit with evidence you already have.

We map your real controls to the framework you are being asked about, find the gaps, and set up evidence collection so the next audit is cheaper.

Discuss Compliance & GRC ▸Open in the interactive menu

Who it's for

  • Businesses facing their first ISO 27001, SOC 2 or customer security questionnaire
  • Organisations whose policies were written once and never used
  • Teams preparing evidence for an audit under time pressure

Problems it helps with

  • Not knowing how far you are from a framework's requirements
  • Policies that don't match how the business really works
  • Evidence scattered across inboxes and screenshots
  • Audits that cost more each year because nothing is reused

Scope and deliverables

A gap assessment against the framework you're being asked about, followed by the policies, owners and evidence tracking needed to close the gaps. Certification itself is issued by an accredited auditor, not by us.

What we do

  • Gap assessment against ISO 27001, CIS or SOC 2
  • Policy set written for your size
  • Evidence collection and control tracking
  • Audit preparation and support

What you get

  • Gap report with owners
  • Policy pack
  • Control and evidence tracker

How an engagement works

  1. Conversation

    You tell us what's going on and what you need. We ask questions to understand your environment and constraints.

  2. Written scope

    We agree in writing what is in and out of scope, what you'll receive and when, before any work starts.

  3. The work

    Carried out as agreed. Anything urgent is raised with you as soon as it's found, not saved for the report.

  4. Report and walkthrough

    Findings in plain language, ranked by what to fix first, and a walkthrough with your team.

Questions

Can you certify us?

No. Certification comes from an independent, accredited auditor. We help you get ready and support you through the audit.

Which framework should we choose?

It depends on who is asking. Customers in some markets expect ISO 27001, others SOC 2, and CIS Controls are a practical baseline when no one has asked yet.

Will we end up with a stack of documents nobody reads?

The aim is the opposite: a policy set sized for your business, written to match what you actually do.
