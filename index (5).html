Network Security

Hard to get into, harder to move through.

Firewalls, segmentation, VPN and wireless, reviewed against how attackers actually move once they are inside.

Discuss Network Security ▸Open in the interactive menu

Who it's for

  • Businesses with firewalls and VPNs that have grown rule by rule over years
  • Organisations with a flat network where everything can reach everything
  • Teams adding remote access for staff or suppliers

Problems it helps with

  • Firewall rules nobody can explain, including rules that allow too much
  • No separation between user devices, servers and sensitive systems
  • VPNs and remote access that rely on passwords alone
  • Guest and corporate wireless that aren't properly separated

Scope and deliverables

A review of your firewalls, segmentation, remote access and wireless against how attackers move once inside, with a prioritised plan you can carry out in stages.

What we do

  • Firewall rule and configuration review
  • Network segmentation design
  • VPN and remote access hardening
  • Wireless security assessment

What you get

  • Rule clean-up list
  • Segmentation plan
  • Network diagram with trust zones

How an engagement works

  1. Conversation

    You tell us what's going on and what you need. We ask questions to understand your environment and constraints.

  2. Written scope

    We agree in writing what is in and out of scope, what you'll receive and when, before any work starts.

  3. The work

    Carried out as agreed. Anything urgent is raised with you as soon as it's found, not saved for the report.

  4. Report and walkthrough

    Findings in plain language, ranked by what to fix first, and a walkthrough with your team.

Questions

Do we need new firewalls?

Rarely. Most improvements come from cleaning up rules and adding segmentation on the equipment you already have.

Will changes cause outages?

Recommendations are staged and tested so that changes can be made one step at a time, with a rollback plan.

Do you need access to our devices?

Read-only exports of configurations are usually enough for the review.
