Mergers & Migrations

Bring an acquired company in without bringing its problems.

Security due diligence, day-one controls and tenant migrations for companies that grow by acquisition.

Discuss Mergers & Migrations ▸Open in the interactive menu

Who it's for

  • Companies acquiring businesses whose security they don't yet know
  • IT teams merging Microsoft 365 tenants after an acquisition
  • Organisations planning day-one controls for a newly acquired company

Problems it helps with

  • Buying a business without knowing what security debt comes with it
  • Connecting networks before the acquired environment is understood
  • Email, domains and identities that need to be merged without disruption
  • No plan for which controls apply from day one

Scope and deliverables

Security due diligence before a deal, day-one and day-100 security plans after it, and the security side of Microsoft 365 tenant, email and identity migrations.

What we do

  • Pre-deal security due diligence
  • Day-one and day-100 security plans
  • Microsoft 365 tenant migration security
  • Email, identity and domain clean-up

What you get

  • Due diligence findings
  • Integration plan by phase
  • Migration cut-over checklist

How an engagement works

  1. Conversation

    You tell us what's going on and what you need. We ask questions to understand your environment and constraints.

  2. Written scope

    We agree in writing what is in and out of scope, what you'll receive and when, before any work starts.

  3. The work

    Carried out as agreed. Anything urgent is raised with you as soon as it's found, not saved for the report.

  4. Report and walkthrough

    Findings in plain language, ranked by what to fix first, and a walkthrough with your team.

Questions

When should security be involved in an acquisition?

Before the deal closes if possible, so findings can inform the price, the terms and the integration plan.

Do you handle the whole tenant migration?

We cover the security side: identities, access, email authentication and data. The migration itself can be run by your team or a migration partner.

What happens on day one?

A short list of controls the acquired company adopts immediately, such as MFA and endpoint protection, before networks are connected.
