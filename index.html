About SecureAxis

A cyber security consultancy that also builds its own security products.

Three practical questions

  1. Who has access to critical systems?
  2. Would we know if an account was compromised?
  3. What should we fix first?

SecureAxis helps businesses answer those three questions across their whole environment, from office IT to industrial control systems.

Our work covers penetration testing and vulnerability management, network security, Linux and Windows hardening, Microsoft 365 and Active Directory security, security monitoring and incident response, and ICS and OT security for industrial sites.

Whether you're trying to understand a recent incident or looking to improve your defenses, we explain what we find in plain language and give your team a clear plan for what to tackle first.

Products

We also build our own security products. The first is Glint: it blanks a confidential document when a phone camera is pointed at the screen, and records the attempt. It closes the one leak path that traditional data loss prevention can't see.

Who's behind SecureAxis

SecureAxis is founder-led and veteran-owned. Our work draws on more than five years of hands-on cybersecurity experience: leading enterprise security operations, advising clients on security architecture, and helping reduce a vulnerability backlog by nearly 30% in one year. We hold Splunk and CompTIA certifications, with further training in security management and NIST frameworks.

Where we work

We work with businesses in the United States and Malaysia, and remotely with clients elsewhere.
