About

Cyber security consulting, end to end

  1. Who has access to critical systems?
  2. Would we know if an account was compromised?
  3. What should we fix first?

SecureAxis is a cyber security consultancy. We help businesses answer those three questions across their whole environment, from office IT to industrial control systems.

Our work covers penetration testing and vulnerability management, network security, Linux and Windows hardening, Microsoft 365 and Active Directory security, security monitoring and incident response, and ICS and OT security for industrial sites.

Who's behind it

SecureAxis is founder-led and veteran-owned. Our work draws on more than five years of hands-on cybersecurity experience: leading enterprise security operations, advising clients on security architecture, and helping reduce a vulnerability backlog by nearly 30% in one year. We hold Splunk and CompTIA certifications, with further training in security management and NIST frameworks.

Glint

          
Stop the photo your DLP can't see.

          
Glint notices when a phone camera is aimed at a confidential document and blanks that document. The rest of the screen stays as it was, and the attempt is recorded.

          
  1. The camera turns on only while a Confidential document is open.
  2. A phone aimed at the screen blanks just that window.
  3. The attempt is logged for your security team. No video is stored.

AI Gateway

          
AI answers are the new phishing email.

          
AI Gateway sits between your people, your apps and AI model APIs, and checks every prompt and every answer for phishing links, hidden instructions, leaked secrets and malicious install commands.

          
  1. Hidden characters are decoded first, so nothing invisible slips through.
  2. Suspicious links and files are opened in a sandbox, and the verdict comes from what happened.
  3. Apps switch over by changing one address. Model API keys stay in the gateway.

SecureAxis Response

          
SecureAxis decides. Your EDR enforces.

          
Response actions through the EDR you already run, each with a reason, a named approver and a record your auditors can read.
