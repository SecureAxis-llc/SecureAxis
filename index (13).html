Endpoint Security

Laptops and servers hardened and watched.

EDR rollouts, baseline hardening and posture checks, done across the whole fleet including the awkward servers nobody wants to reboot.

Discuss Endpoint Security ▸Open in the interactive menu

Who it's for

  • Businesses rolling out or replacing EDR (endpoint detection and response)
  • IT teams managing Windows and macOS fleets with inconsistent settings
  • Organisations where many users still have local admin rights

Problems it helps with

  • Devices missing protection, encryption or updates without anyone noticing
  • Inconsistent security settings across the fleet
  • Admin rights that make every phishing email more dangerous
  • Exceptions that were meant to be temporary and became permanent

Scope and deliverables

Covers endpoint protection, hardening baselines, disk encryption and admin rights across your laptops, desktops and servers, using the management tools you already run.

What we do

  • EDR selection, rollout and vendor swaps
  • CIS baseline hardening for Windows and macOS
  • Disk encryption, admin rights and posture checks
  • Exception handling that does not rot

What you get

  • Fleet posture scorecard
  • Hardening baseline as policy
  • Rollout plan with rollback steps

How an engagement works

  1. Conversation

    You tell us what's going on and what you need. We ask questions to understand your environment and constraints.

  2. Written scope

    We agree in writing what is in and out of scope, what you'll receive and when, before any work starts.

  3. The work

    Carried out as agreed. Anything urgent is raised with you as soon as it's found, not saved for the report.

  4. Report and walkthrough

    Findings in plain language, ranked by what to fix first, and a walkthrough with your team.

Questions

Which EDR product do you recommend?

The one that fits your environment, budget and team. We help compare options against your requirements and plan the rollout.

Will hardening break our applications?

Baselines are tested with a pilot group first, and genuine exceptions are documented with a reason and an owner.

Do you cover macOS?

Yes, Windows and macOS fleets, plus servers.
